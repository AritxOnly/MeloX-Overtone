# Overtone Release Notes / 弦外更新日志

## iOS + Apple Watch

### 简体中文

- 「我的消息」采用更紧凑的会话列表。聊天时底栏渐隐，返回时渐显，点按顶部头像或昵称可查看对方主页。
- 「评论和 @提及」可在标题栏下方随时切换。点开评论或点赞通知可直接查看对应评论，歌曲评论区会补全歌名、歌手和封面。
- 播放页支持全屏动态封面，顶部和底部延续封面色彩。从歌词页切回或回到前台时，封面可继续播放。
- 游客可直接打开个人页，并选择短信、二维码或网页登录。
- 音乐库新增「最近在听」，优化播放列表、艺人、云盘和下载页，并缓存歌曲、专辑与艺人列表的排序结果。
- 歌曲行支持滑动排入下一首、下载及移除。播放时封面显示五条实时波形，暂停时保留暗色封面和收平的波形。
- 在「设置 → 通用」可分别切换强调色和 App 图标。歌词翻译可直接开关，系统播放控件也可收藏歌曲。

### English

- Browse conversations in a denser inbox. The tab bar fades out when you open a chat and back in when you leave. Tap the avatar or name to view the sender’s profile.
- Switch between comments and mentions below the title bar. Open a comment or like notification to jump to the relevant comment, with the song title, artist and artwork loaded in the sheet.
- View full-screen animated artwork with colors that extend above and below the cover. Animation resumes when you return from lyrics or bring the app back to the foreground.
- Open the profile as a guest and choose SMS, QR code or web sign-in.
- Open recently played albums and playlists from the library. Updated playlists, artists, cloud music and downloads, with cached sorting for songs, albums and artists.
- Swipe song rows to play next, download or remove. Five live audio bands appear over the current artwork and settle flat when paused.
- Choose the accent color and app icon separately in General settings. Toggle lyric translation directly and favorite songs from the system playback controls.
